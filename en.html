Privacy Policy

App: Noctivo · com.noctivo.player
Effective and last updated: October 13, 2026.

Noctivo is an Android video player. Your library, settings and playback history are primarily processed on your device. Purchases use Google Play, and torrent downloads exchange data with the BitTorrent network. This policy explains those activities.

On-device dataNetwork featuresData deletionContact

1. Developer and scope

This policy covers Noctivo, developed by ForgeGamesStudio. You do not need a Noctivo account or a separate Google sign-in to use the player. Google Play uses your Google Play account to process and restore purchases.

Privacy contact: forgegamesstudio@gmail.com.

2. Data processed on your device

  • Videos and subtitles: files you select, names, locations or URIs, sizes, duration, technical metadata and thumbnails, used to discover, display, play and preview your media. Imported subtitles may be copied into private app storage.
  • Settings and history: language, appearance, playback preferences, resume positions, recent videos, bookmarks, lists, subtitle profiles and viewing statistics, stored locally for these features.
  • Downloads: torrent files or magnet links you add, selected files, destination folders, task progress and status.
  • Purchases: product identifiers, purchase tokens, status and signed Google Play receipts. Verified receipts are cached locally to provide PRO access, including offline access.
  • Diagnostics: local error logs may include timestamps, app and device technical details, error messages, and file names or paths included in an error.

The app does not automatically send your media library, viewing history or local logs to the developer. The current version has no advertising SDK, third-party analytics, or automatic crash-report uploads to a developer server.

3. Android permissions

Access to videos and selected files or folders enables media discovery and playback. Optional “All files access,” if granted, enables discovery of videos and nearby subtitles in accessible shared storage. It does not give access to other apps’ private data.

The app uses internet access for network features and Google Play, network-state information to manage downloads, and notifications and a foreground service to run and display downloads. On older Android versions, storage-write permission is used to save files. You can revoke permissions in Android settings; related features may then become unavailable.

4. Torrents and network exchange

When you add or start a torrent download, the libtorrent library connects to BitTorrent peers, trackers and peer-discovery services, including DHT. Your IP address, network port, torrent identifier (info hash), client information and exchanged file pieces may be visible to those participants. The protocol may upload pieces of the downloading files to other peers while a task is active.

This exchange supports your requested download and is not anonymous. ForgeGamesStudio does not control independent network participants or their retention practices. Stop the relevant downloads to stop this exchange. Local playback alone does not require joining the torrent network.

5. Google Play purchases

Pro-Lite and PRO are one-time purchases unlocking the same features. PRO provides additional support for development. Google Play processes payment information and associates purchases with your account. Noctivo does not receive your full bank-card number or Google account password.

The app contacts Google Play for prices, purchases, acknowledgement and restoration. The current version verifies receipt signatures on the device and does not use a separate developer purchase-verification server. Successful refreshes update entitlement status; offline access may rely on a previously verified receipt.

Google’s processing is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Backups and error reports

You can export settings and related user data to a backup file in a location you choose, including a cloud provider selected in the system picker. Exported files are not automatically sent to the developer.

Depending on device settings, Android may back up some app data or transfer it to another device. Cached purchase receipts are excluded from Android backup and device transfer. Purchases are restored through Google Play.

Error reports are saved or shared only when you request it. You can review a report and remove personal details before sending it. If you contact the developer or send a report, your sender address, message and attachments are processed to respond and resolve the issue. Correspondence uses Google email services; the service you choose to share a report also applies its own privacy policy.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Local preferences, history, cache, imported subtitles and task data remain until removed using available app controls or by clearing Noctivo’s storage in Android settings. Uninstalling normally removes private app data, although Android backup may restore some data on reinstallation.
  • The in-app settings reset is not a complete data deletion and may preserve history, lists and other user data. Use Android’s “Clear storage / Clear data” to erase local app data.
  • Original media, downloaded files, exported backups and reports in shared storage or cloud storage may remain after uninstalling. Delete them separately where stored. Manage system backups in Android or the relevant account settings.
  • Local error logs are size-limited and older entries are replaced through log rotation. Clearing private app data also removes them.
  • Correspondence voluntarily sent to the developer is retained for as long as needed to handle the request and related issue, or longer if legally required. You may request deletion using the contact email.
  • Uninstalling does not erase Google’s purchase records or cancel a purchase. Google Play’s rules govern payment-data retention and refunds.

8. Security and your choices

Local service data uses app storage and Android access controls. File access depends on permissions granted. Digital signatures are checked for purchase receipts. These measures cannot guarantee absolute protection on a compromised device or a modified app.

You can revoke permissions, stop downloads, delete local data, and contact us about information you voluntarily sent to the developer. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of that information. The developer cannot remotely read or delete your local media library.

9. This website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no developer analytics scripts, advertising trackers, data-collection forms or cookies. GitHub may process request information, including your IP address, to provide and secure hosting. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

10. Changes and contact

We will update this policy and its date when features or data practices change. Material changes requiring separate notice or consent will be accompanied by an appropriate in-app notification.
